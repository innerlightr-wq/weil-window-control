== 2. structural identities (the whole content of the mean triple) ==
  (i)   A == 1/2 identically                  -> carries no information about delta
  (ii)  G^2 == A * M_H  (AM.HM = GM^2)        -> G and M_H are algebraically equivalent
  (iii) hence  M_H = 2 G^2  and  D = A - M_H = 2 delta^2
  => the mean triple (A,G,M_H) is a function of the SINGLE scalar D, and D = 2 delta^2.
     No mean gap carries information beyond squared zero displacement.

== 3. the three gaps as functions of D on 0 <= D <= 1/2 ==
  A - M_H = D                                      (identity; strictly increasing)
  A - G   = 1/2 - sqrt(1/4 - D/2)                   (strictly increasing, convex)
  G - M_H = sqrt(1/4 - D/2) - 1/2 + D               (NOT monotone)

  critical point   D = 3/8   (delta = sqrt(3)/4 = 0.4330127..., beta = 0.9330127...)
  there  G = 1/4 exactly, and  max (G - M_H) = 1/8  exactly
  G - M_H = 0 at D = 0    (equality p = q = 1/2, the RH point):      PASS
  G - M_H = 0 at D = 1/2  (complete dominance, beta in {0,1}, G = M_H = 0): PASS
  d^2/dD^2 (G - M_H) = -(1/16)(1/4 - D/2)^{-3/2} < 0  -> strictly concave,
  so G - M_H is two-to-one onto (0, 1/8): it is a STRICTLY WORSE diagnostic than D.

  two-to-one, exhibited: solve sqrt(1/4 - D/2) = v + 1/2 - D for v = 1/10
    D^2 - (7/10) D + (11/100) = 0,  discriminant = 1/20 > 0  -> two roots in (0, 1/2)
    D = 0.2381966011 and 0.4618033989   (delta = 0.3451062163 and 0.4805223194)

== 4. along any specified evolution tau |-> D(tau) ==
  (A - M_H)' = D'                      -- always the sign of D'
  (A - G)'   = D'/(4G)                 -- always the sign of D'  (G > 0)
  (G - M_H)' = D' (1 - 1/(4G))         -- sign of D' iff G > 1/4 iff D < 3/8
  So for D > 3/8 the gap G - M_H moves OPPOSITE to the displacement diagnostic.

== 5. the arc coordinate, and why M_H'' = 1 - 4 M_H is not a restoring law ==
  put delta = (1/2) sin(theta).  Then M_H = 1/2 - 2 delta^2 = 1/4 + (1/4) cos(2 theta),
  so  d^2 M_H/d theta^2 = -cos(2 theta) = 1 - 4 M_H.   TRUE, and empty:
  theta is a coordinate on the fixed configuration, not a time.  NEGATIVE CONTROL:
  every function of the form  c0 + c1 cos(2 theta)  satisfies  f'' = -4(f - c0),
  so the 'oscillator' form is a property of 'quadratic in sin theta', not of zeta.
  checked at three theta for three (c0,c1): max residual < 1e-12  -> PASS
