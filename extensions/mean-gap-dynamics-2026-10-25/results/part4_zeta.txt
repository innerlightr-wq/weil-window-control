   WHAT THIS IS NOT.  It is tempting to say the emergent pair is the only
   non-real pair, hence extremal, hence (S1c) FORCES D' <= -1.  That reading
   is WRONG: see src/zeta_lehmer.py.  Adjacent zeta gaps shrink like
   2 pi/log(gamma/2 pi) and a pair of gap g in z collides at tau ~ -g^2/8, so
   the Lehmer pair (6709/6710) leaves the axis at tau ~ -7.1e-4 against
   ~ -1.04 here -- a factor 1460.  Zeros leave in order of INCREASING gap, so
   by tau ~ -1.28 this low-height pair is one of the LAST to leave and is
   certainly not extremal.  The D' <= -1 above is a numerical fact CONSISTENT
   with the lemma, not an instance of it.  (That the competing deeper zeros
   sit at gamma ~ 7000 while this pair is at gamma ~ 77 is also why their
   positive pull is ~1e-8: see the separation bound (S1d),
