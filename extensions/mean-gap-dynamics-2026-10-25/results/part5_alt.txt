   VERDICT on the kappa-family: REJECTED, on a checkable criterion.
   * what varies:      kappa, a genuine arithmetic parameter (Gauss-sum derived)
   * what is preserved: the Dirichlet series, its periodicity mod 5, its
                        abscissa of convergence -- and NOTHING ELSE
   * functional equation: holds ONLY at kappa = kappa_DH.  Moving kappa by 1e-3
                        already breaks Lambda(s) = Lambda(1-s) at the 1e-3 level,
                        and at kappa = 0.2 by O(1).  So off the DH point there is
                        no completed function, no explicit formula, no spectral
                        side, and no finite-window Weil form at all.
   * does zeta occur at a specified parameter?  NO.  zeta is not in the family at
                        any kappa (conductor 1 vs 5, and zeta has a pole).
   * are the zeros zeros of a deformed function?  They are zeros of an actual
                        Dirichlet series, but of one with no functional equation,
                        so they are not the spectrum of any Weil form.
   * what inference could return to zeta?  NONE.  There is no parameter path from
                        the family to zeta, so no limit argument is available.

   Three of the brief's five criteria fail outright.  The branch is CLOSED.

   No second alternative is pursued.  The repository's own prior findings already
   cover the obvious ones: extensions/source-flow-detector-audit-2026-10-24 Sec.7
   records that no appropriate prime-side representation of the deformed family
   has been established at tau != 0, and its Phase-H experiment (parent
   extensions/davenport-heilbronn-2026-10-23 Sec.15) records that splitting the
   arithmetic side by the support of b_n does not separate 'has off-line zeros'
   from 'has no Euler product'.  Nothing here reopens either.
