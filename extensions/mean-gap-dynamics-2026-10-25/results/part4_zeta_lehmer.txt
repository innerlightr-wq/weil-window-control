   fact CONSISTENT with the lemma, not an instance of it.  Combined with the
   Davenport-Heilbronn side -- where O_1 is extremal only within a finite census
   and O_2..O_4 are provably not extremal -- the position is uniform:

     IN NEITHER CONTROL IS THE ONE SIGN-FORCING HYPOTHESIS VERIFIABLE.

   This is not a defect of the computation.  Extremality is a statement about
   ALL zeros, so no finite computation can establish it; and that is exactly why
   the forced-sign statement integrates only to the global strip bound.
